● You're on the right track. ✅ Here's the full reason it's really bad, not just
  slow.

  The robot has one cashier handling everything: the drivetrain, the intake, the
  shooter, the logging. Every 20 ms, the cashier serves each subsystem quickly
  and moves on. A while loop that waits for a button is like one customer saying
  "hold on, let me find my wallet…" while the line stops completely:

  - The other subsystems' periodic() methods never run.
  - No new commands start, and the dashboard stops updating.
  - The motors are stuck doing whatever they were last told. If the robot was
    driving at full speed, it keeps going. 😬

  loop") and never wait. That's what triggers like controller.a().whileTrue(...)

  New idea: IO layers (C12)
